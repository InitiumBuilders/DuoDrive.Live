# DuoDrive.Live

> Code And Vibe. The platform to build live, and build together. Build your product live. Because two is better than one.

**[duodrive.live](https://duodrive.live)**

---

DuoDrive.Live is the open-source live-streaming platform for Vibe Coders. Two builders pair up — one Pirate (prompts, direction), one Refiner (code, ship) — and broadcast their build session live, side by side. The community joins the room as a Cortex, claiming helper roles. VOTUS is the building-and-voting token (preview).

## Core concepts

- **Avari Sync** — your vibe-coding partner. The duo is the unit, not the individual.
- **Initium** — a project-in-flight. Every Initium has at least two signers and a Two-Key Drive vault.
- **Two-Key Drive** — files unlock when both partners sign with Dash EVO ID + ZK proof. The literal product behind the metaphor.
- **Community Cortex** — self-organizing helper protocol. Roles are claimed, not assigned.
- **Avari Signal** — direction-only voting. Tallies hide until the duo opens the box. Whispers > shouts.
- **Forge Meter** — daily momentum signal across commits, polls, joins, and VOTUS staked.
- **VOTUS** — building-and-voting token on Dash Platform (preview).

## Surprise features (high-leverage)

- **The Forge Meter** — visual proof of momentum. Resets daily. Streaks compound.
- **Two-Key Drive** — the vault demo: files unlock only when both partners sign.
- **Avari Echo** — when one builder says something profound on stream, the AI tags it; the other partner gets a "your partner just said something worth keeping" notification.
- **The Shadow Stream** — a third feed that shows nothing but the live diff. Watchers see the building, not the talking.
- **VOTUS Whispers** — votes show direction (the Avari Signal), revealing the final tally only when the duo asks. Removes bandwagon bias.

## Stack

- Next.js 15 · App Router · TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS · custom Forge palette
- Framer Motion (lightweight)
- Mobile-first, neo-glass minimal, dark with twin neon accents

## Status

This is the V1 scaffold and full UI/UX preview — frontend prototype, deliberately backend-light. The interactions, design system, and emotional arc are working. Real auth, streaming relay, Dash Platform integration, and Two-Key Drive vault are next.
